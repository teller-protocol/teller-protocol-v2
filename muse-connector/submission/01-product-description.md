## Connector name

    Teller

## One-line description

    Prequalify for personal, business, home, and auto loans.

## Category

Finance / Lending

## Long description

> Teller helps you find out which lenders will actually work with you, before you
> apply anywhere. Tell Muse what you need — a personal loan, working capital for
> your business, a mortgage or HELOC, a car loan or an auto refinance — and Teller
> checks your details against its lender network and reports who would match, what
> rate range to expect, and how much you could borrow.
>
> Previewing is free and private: it stores nothing and sends nothing to any
> lender, so you can change an answer and look again as many times as you like.
> Nothing reaches a lender until you say so explicitly.
>
> Prequalification uses a soft credit check and does not affect your credit score.

## What users will do with it

1. **Find out what they qualify for.** "What kind of personal loan could I get with
   a 710 score and $85k income?" Teller returns matching lenders with estimated
   APR and amount ranges. Nothing is stored or sent.
2. **Compare products.** "Is a HELOC or a cash-out refi better for $60k?" The
   connector reports which lenders serve each product for their situation.
3. **Shop for a car or home loan** before walking into a dealership or making an
   offer, so they know their real budget.
4. **Apply once instead of five times.** When they're ready, one confirmed
   submission routes their details to every matching lender at once, rather than
   filling in the same form on five different sites.

## Markets at launch

United States and Canada. Every other country returns a clear "not available
yet" rather than a partial experience.

## Pricing to the user

Free. Teller is paid by the lender side, not the borrower. The connector has no
paywall, no subscription and no per-call charge to the end user.
